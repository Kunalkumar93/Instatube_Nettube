# 🎬 InstaTube & NetTube

> **Turn YouTube into Instagram or Netflix with just one click.**

A lightweight, zero-bloat Chrome extension that restyles your entire YouTube browsing experience into pixel-perfect **Instagram** or **Netflix** interfaces using YouTube's real data.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start (How to Use)

You can set this up in Chrome in less than 60 seconds:

### Step 1: Download or Clone
Download this repository as a ZIP (and extract it), or clone it via terminal:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Kunalkumar93/Instatube_Nettube.git
```

### Step 2: Load into Chrome
1. Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions` in your URL bar.
2. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left.
4. Select the **`Instatube_Nettube`** folder.

### Step 3: Enjoy YouTube!
1. Open [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) (or refresh it if already open).
2. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar (or use the on-screen button) to switch between **Instagram Mode** and **Netflix Mode**!

---

## 🎮 How to Switch Themes

You have two easy ways to switch anytime:

1. **On-Screen Button (Fastest)**: Look at the top right of the page — click the **Instagram Mode** button to switch to Instagram, or click **Netflix Mode** in the left sidebar to switch to Netflix.
2. **From the Extension Popup**: Click the extension icon in Chrome's top toolbar, then click the **Instagram** or **Netflix** card.

## ✨ Features

### 📸 Instagram Mode ("InstaTube")
- **Stories Tray**: Channels and subscriptions appear with Instagram's signature gradient rings at the top.
- **Clean Post Feed**: Centered feed cards (~470px width) with creator avatars, verified badges, and captions.
- **Double-Tap to Like**: Double-click any video image to trigger an animated Instagram heart burst and like the post!
- **Interactive Action Bar**: Like heart, comment button, share link (copies to clipboard), and bookmark/save.
- **Live Comments**: Type into the *"Add a comment..."* box and post comments directly on the page.
- **Reels Mode**: Switch to a full-screen vertical swipeable Reels feed with audio vinyl and action buttons.
- **Suggestions Sidebar**: Recommended channels with clickable "Follow" buttons.

### 🍿 Netflix Mode ("NetTube")
- **Hero Billboard**: Cinematic full-width featured video with background gradient fades.
- **Cinema Rows**: "Trending Now", "From Your Subscriptions", "Popular on YouTube", and more.
- **Top 10 Badges**: Numbered rank badges just like Netflix Top 10.
- **Hover Previews**: Hover over any video to preview duration, play buttons, and tags.

---

## 💡 Tips & Troubleshooting

- **Don't see the new UI right away?** Simply press **`F5`** or **`Ctrl + R`** to refresh your YouTube tab after loading the extension.
- **Watching a video**: When you click to play any video, YouTube's standard video player opens normally so you don't miss out on video quality, comments, or playback controls.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Built using **Manifest V3**.
- Pure JavaScript and Vanilla CSS — zero external frameworks or heavy dependencies.
- Completely client-side and privacy-friendly (no tracking, uses YouTube's native data).
